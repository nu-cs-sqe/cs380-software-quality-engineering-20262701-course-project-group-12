# Chess Rules

The rules of play are the [FIDE Laws of Chess](https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/e012023), in force from 1 January 2023.

The playable requirements are the user stories in [user-stories.md](user-stories.md). Each story is one task on the project management board. The Week 4 setup task is also written out in [game-setup.md](game-setup.md).

The application uses the Basic Rules of Play and the draw rules. It has no chess clock, and a move is made by clicking a piece and then its destination square.
